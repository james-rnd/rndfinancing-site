FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

What exactly do you lend against?

Accrued and filed SR&ED claims — the refundable investment tax credit a company has earned through eligible R&D spend. We advance a portion of the expected refund and are repaid when the CRA pays it out.

What happens if the CRA is slow, or reduces the claim?

CRA timing risk is ours to manage — that's the product. Facilities are sized conservatively against each claim precisely so that a review or an adjustment doesn't put your company in a hard place. Specific terms are set out plainly in each facility agreement.

What does it cost?

Pricing is stated on one page in every offer: the rate, any fees, and the total cost in dollars. No sliding scales discovered after signing. [Indicative pricing to be published.]

Do you take equity, warrants, or personal guarantees?

No equity, no warrants. Security is registered against the claim proceeds themselves. Anything beyond that is disclosed up front in the term sheet, before you commit to anything.

I'm a consultant — how do I actually work with you?

A short conversation, then a written referral arrangement with disclosed economics. You introduce clients who need their refund sooner; we handle the lending and keep you informed. You remain the claim expert throughout.

Who is behind RND Financing?

RND Financing is backed by Cashcade, an established R&D lender. We're building our Canadian presence through the consultant community. Questions? Email james@rndfinancing.com — you'll get James, not a ticket queue.
